# What a wrong Mooods page looks like

Concrete rejections. If a proposal matches the left column, it is not Mooods — no matter
how well executed.

## Copy

| Rejected | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Bienvenue sur Mooods Studio, votre partenaire créatif en 3D et IA." | Agency voice. Mooods has no clients here. | "Je construis des choses pour voir jusqu'où elles peuvent aller." |
| "Découvrez Ruinator, l'addon révolutionnaire qui transforme vos scènes en un clic !" | Guru voice, superlatives, exclamation. | "Ruinator 0.3 est en ligne. Les murs se fissurent bien, les portes traversent encore le sol." |
| "Voici la meilleure façon d'utiliser l'IA pour créer en 3D." | Claims mastery. | "J'ai testé cette méthode pour voir si l'IA pouvait m'aider à construire la scène." |
| "Nos services : modélisation 3D, rendu, motion design, branding." | A service list. There are no services. | The five verbs: what interests me, what I build, what I test, what I learn, what I share. |
| "Rejoignez une communauté de créateurs passionnés." | Filler that could describe any site. | "Suis le build pendant qu'il se fait." |
| "12 projets · 3 ans d'expérience · ∞ idées" | Fabricated stats, data slop. | Nothing. Or one real number if it means something. |
| "Explorez l'univers infini de la créativité augmentée." | Words with no referent. | Name the actual thing: a scene, an addon, a game, a note. |

## Structure

- **A page made only of card grids.** Three rows of identical cards with an icon, a title
  and two lines. See the alternation rule in `LAYOUT.md`.
- **A features section.** Mooods has no features. It has projects, experiments and notes.
- **A testimonials or client-logos band.** There are no clients and no testimonials to show.
- **A pricing table.** Nothing is sold on this site.
- **A hero at `100vh` with a centred headline and two buttons.** Size the hero to its content
  and let the scene carry it.
- **Numbered markers (01 / 02 / 03)** on things that are not a sequence. The numbered
  chapters of the home ARE a sequence — the reader walks down a path — and the Field Notes
  are numbered because the numbering is real. A row of feature cards is not a sequence.
- **An icon grid** where each icon is a generic glyph, used as the CONTENT of a section.
  A single row of line icons labelling what the workshop covers, at the foot of a scene, is
  part of the style — a full grid of icons in circles standing in for a section is not.

## Visual

- Purple-to-blue gradient hero. Neon cyberpunk city as decoration. Chrome, mirror metal,
  glossy plastic.
- A straight horizontal line between a scene band and a paper band. The transition is a
  torn paper edge — see `STYLE-carnet.md`.
- Fast or showy motion: parallax that jumps, sparkles, lens flares, animated gradients.
  Movement is allowed and wanted, but at the speed of a slow breath — `LAYOUT.md` has the
  numbers.
- Emoji as section markers or in tags.
- One radius and one shadow on every block, so nothing has hierarchy.
- Nook posing at the camera, smiling, thumbs up, or with the goggles pushed up on his
  forehead. Nook and the wordmark stacked on top of each other.
- A rounded card with a coloured left border accent. Text over an unveiled photo.
- Light cyan or light orange text on the light background.
- More than one warm accent and one cool accent competing on the same screen.

## The test

Before shipping, answer the five questions in `README.md`. The fifth one decides:
**does it make you want to explore?** If the page reads as something to be informed by
rather than something to poke around in, it has failed, whatever else is right.
